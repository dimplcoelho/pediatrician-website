Project Scope – Personal Pediatrician Website
1. Project Overview
The project involves developing a modern, responsive personal website for a pediatrician to establish their professional online presence and provide parents with reliable child-health information.

The website will showcase the doctor's qualifications, experience, professional profile and healthcare expertise. It will also serve as an educational platform where parents can access doctor-reviewed health guides, nutrition information, vaccination guidance, child development resources and educational videos.

A simple, secure doctor dashboard will allow the pediatrician to manage website content and share relevant health resources with parents through WhatsApp or direct links.

The website is intended for one pediatrician's personal professional identity, not as a hospital or multi-doctor management system.
2. Project Objectives
- Establish a professional online identity for the pediatrician.
- Build trust and credibility with parents.
- Make child-health information easily accessible.
- Provide educational resources in English and Kannada.
- Allow parents to access shared health guides without registration.
- Enable the doctor to manage and update website content independently.
- Improve communication by sharing reusable educational resources.
3. Scope of the Public Website
A. Home Page
- Doctor's introduction and professional photograph.
- Welcome message.
- Qualifications and experience highlights.
- Featured child-health resources.
- Popular health topics.
- Featured YouTube videos.
- Contact and clinic information.
B. About the Doctor
- Doctor's profile and photograph.
- Educational qualifications.
- Professional experience.
- Specialisation.
- Professional journey.
- Approach towards child healthcare.
C. Child Health Information
A dedicated section for educational articles and guides.

Categories may include:
- Common Childhood Illnesses.
- Fever, Cough & Cold.
- Digestion and Stomach Health.
- Skin Conditions and Allergies.
- Vaccination Guidance.
- General Child Care.
Each health guide will have its own shareable page.
D. Nutrition & Growth
Educational resources covering:
- Infant feeding.
- Toddler nutrition.
- Healthy eating habits.
- Picky eating.
- Healthy food recommendations.
- Physical growth.
- Developmental milestones.
- Sleep and behavioural development.
E. Video Library
- Integration of the doctor's YouTube videos.
- Video categories and titles.
- Video descriptions.
- Embedded video playback.
- Links to the doctor's YouTube channel.
F. Contact Page
- Clinic address.
- Contact number.
- Consultation timings.
- Google Maps integration.
- WhatsApp contact option.
- Social media links.
G. Multilingual Support
- English language.
- Kannada language.
- Language switcher across the website.
- Translated health articles and website content.
4. Doctor Admin Dashboard
A simple, secure dashboard will be developed for the doctor to manage website information.
Module	Functionalities
Dashboard	Overview of published guides, drafts and quick actions
Health Guides	Create, edit, preview, publish and unpublish articles
Categories	Add, edit and organise health categories
Videos	Add, update and remove YouTube video links
Doctor Profile	Update photograph, qualifications, experience and introduction
Website Settings	Manage contact information, clinic details and language content

Health Guide Management
The doctor will be able to:
- Create a new health guide.
- Enter English and Kannada content.
- Assign a category and optional age group.
- Add relevant YouTube videos.
- Preview articles before publishing.
- Save articles as drafts.
- Publish or unpublish guides.
- Edit previously published guides.
5. Health Guide Sharing Functionality
Core feature
A dedicated sharing feature will allow the doctor to share educational resources directly with parents.

Workflow:
1. Doctor logs into the dashboard.
2. Opens the health guide library.
3. Selects the appropriate educational guide.
4. Selects English or Kannada.
5. Copies the guide URL or shares it through WhatsApp.
6. Parent opens the link without registration or login.
Additional functionality:
- Permanent shareable URLs.
- Mobile-friendly guide pages.
- Language-specific sharing.
- Only published articles accessible publicly.
The doctor will select the appropriate educational material for each child. Shared guides will not replace individual medical consultation or treatment advice.

10. Features Outside the Current Scope
To keep the first version simple and cost-effective, the following are not included:
- Patient registration and login.
- Individual patient medical records.
- Electronic prescriptions.
- Online consultation or video calling.
- Appointment booking and scheduling.
- Billing and payment processing.
- Hospital management functionality.
- Pharmacy management.
- Automated diagnosis or treatment recommendations.
- Patient-specific medication or dosage generation.
These can be considered as future enhancements if required.
11. Proposed Development Phases
Phase	Work
Phase 1	Requirement finalisation and UI/UX design
Phase 2	Public website frontend development
Phase 3	Backend, database and admin dashboard
Phase 4	Health guide and video management
Phase 5	Language integration and sharing functionality
Phase 6	Testing, deployment and handover

12. Expected Outcome
The completed system will provide the pediatrician with a professional online presence and an easy-to-manage educational platform.

Parents will be able to discover the doctor, browse child-health information, watch educational videos and access relevant guidance shared directly by the doctor.

The doctor will have control over published content without needing technical assistance for routine updates.
